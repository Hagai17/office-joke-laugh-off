# Candidates — One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off (u-suzy)

Judge brief (from the kit, GLM-5.3-flash, criteria in order):
1. It really is a one-line office joke. 2. A real laugh — sharp, surprising
punchline, not a mild observation. 3. Clear on one read. 4. Fresh — no office
clichés. 5. Tight wording. Exact ties break toward the smaller text.

Field notes (99 judged texts sampled live from the submissions API): the
crowded clusters are printer jams (≈8 lines), "we're a family" (4), raise →
standing desk (3, near-duplicates), raise → pizza party (2), "per my last
email" (4), meetings about meetings (≈5). Anything in those clusters starts
with a freshness handicap, so all drafts below avoid them.
